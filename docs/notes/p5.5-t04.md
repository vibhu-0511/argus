## What was stored

- `packages/skills/trajectories/_unresolved/mut-0002/verdict.json`.

`diff.patch` is the model's edit measured against the **bug state** (HEAD plus `bug.patch`), not against HEAD. The driver's old behaviour (`git diff` against HEAD) gives an empty file when a fix undoes the bug exactly, which is what a good fix of a mutant does. Variant T takes `git stash create` right after the bug is applied and diffs against that.

## Quota and the "daily budget" criterion

Claude sessions have **no provider cap and no provider ledger**. The criterion "quota stayed within the daily budget, logged by the ledger" is met **only in the self-imposed sense**: the driver's `eval.t14_turn.SELF_IMPOSED` (300 requests and 10 million tokens per model, not a provider limit and not in `docs/quotas.md`). The 21 finished runs used 109 requests (36% of 300) and 274,440 estimated tokens (2.7% of 10 million). That budget never binds: the driver builds a fresh in-memory ledger on every turn. The "ledger" for this task is `packages/skills/trajectories/ledger.json`, built from the trajectories after the fact, not a live ledger that could have refused a call.
